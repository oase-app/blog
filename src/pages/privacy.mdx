This Privacy Policy explains how Our Oase ApS ("we", "us", or "Oase") handles personal data when you use the Oase app and related services.

The short version: Oase is built so that we can read as little of your data as possible. Your messages, posts, names and files are encrypted on your device before they ever reach us, and the encryption keys are kept on separate infrastructure in the EU. We don't sell your data, we don't use it for marketing, and we don't track you.

## Our role: sometimes processor, sometimes controller

Who is "responsible" for your data in the GDPR sense depends on how you use Oase:

- **Oases that belong to a Plenum** (a paying organisation, such as a bostED, school or association): the organisation is the **data controller** for the profile and content data of its members, and we act as their **data processor**. This relationship is governed by our Data Processing Agreement. If you are a member of such an oase, requests about your data should as a starting point go to that organisation — they decide how the oase is used.
- **Oases without a Plenum** (free, independent oases): we are the **data controller** for the profile and content data shared there.
- **Billing and payer contact data**: we are an **independent data controller** — see "If you pay for Oase" below.

## What we collect

- **Profile information**: your name, an optional profile picture, and your birth year and month (we deliberately don't ask for the full date).
- **Content you share**: messages, posts, events, polls, images, files and similar — encrypted on your device before it is sent to us.
- **Payer contact data**: if you or your organisation pays for Oase, we store the organisation's name, contact person and email address (see below).
- **Error reports**: if the app crashes or misbehaves, a technical error report is sent to our error-reporting service so we can fix the problem. Personal-data identifiers are stripped before the report leaves the app — what remains are pseudonymous technical identifiers, never your content.

## What we don't collect

We think it's just as important to be clear about what we *don't* do:

- No phone numbers.
- No full date of birth — only year and month.
- No persistent logging of your IP address.
- No location data.
- No third-party analytics or tracking in the app. We use only our own internal analytics and the error reporting described above.

## Encryption

All user content — messages, posts, names, images, files and associated metadata — is encrypted **on your device** using AES-GCM before it leaves it. What our servers and hosting providers store is ciphertext they cannot read.

- **Media and files** have been client-side encrypted since March 2026. Media uploaded before then was stored unencrypted with Cloudflare, Inc. (US) and is being migrated and phased out.
- **Audio and video calls** are end-to-end encrypted.
- **Encryption keys** are stored exclusively with Scalingo SAS in France — never with US-based services — and are only made available to users who are members of the oase the data was shared in. Our employees cannot read your content.
- **Deletion** happens by *crypto-shredding*: when data is deleted, we destroy the encryption keys, which makes the encrypted data permanently unreadable.

## Where your data is stored

All personal data is **stored** in EU data-centre regions:

- **Database**: AWS Frankfurt/Paris (via Supabase)
- **Files and media**: Scaleway, Paris
- **Application**: Google Cloud Belgium (via Gigalixir)
- **Encryption keys**: Scalingo, Strasbourg

There is one explicit exception: media uploaded before April 2026 is stored with Cloudflare, Inc. in the US. It is being migrated and phased out.

Two kinds of data pass *through* services outside the EU without being stored there:

- **Push notifications** transit Apple, Google and Expo (US) with an encrypted payload that is only decrypted on your device.
- **Technical error reports** are sent to Sentry (US). Personal-data identifiers are stripped before a report leaves the app.

Finally, some of the companies we use are established outside the EU (Gigalixir in the US, Supabase in Singapore) even though the data itself sits in EU regions. Where a provider is subject to third-country legislation, our transfer basis is the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), supplemented by client-side encryption as a supplementary measure in line with EDPB Recommendations 01/2020. The encryption keys never leave the EU, so providers outside the EU only ever handle data they cannot decrypt.

## Logging in

You log in to Oase with [Promise Authentication](https://promiseauthentication.org), an independent login service — think "Log in with Google", but privacy-first. Promise is an independent data controller for the login process. Your email address is handled by Promise, not by us, and Oase never stores it in readable form. You can read more about [how Promise handles data](https://promiseauthentication.org/about/data).

## If you pay for Oase (billing data)

When an organisation buys an Oase Plenum subscription or someone makes a contribution, we store the organisation's name, the contact person's name and an email address. Unlike your content in the app, this is stored in plain text — we need to be able to read it to send invoices and administer the subscription.

For this data, we are an independent data controller:

- **Purpose**: invoicing, receipts, and administering the customer relationship.
- **Legal basis**: performance of a contract (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)) and our legitimate interest in administering the customer relationship (Article 6(1)(f)).
- **Retention**: for as long as the customer relationship lasts, and thereafter as required by Danish bookkeeping law (currently five years from the end of the financial year).
- **Your rights**: all the rights described below apply — contact us directly at [dpa@oase.app](mailto:dpa@oase.app).

## What we won't do with your data

- We will not sell your data.
- We will not use your data for marketing.
- We will not use your data for profiling or automated decision-making.
- Your content is only shared with the other members of the oase you shared it in.

## Cookies and tracking

We do not use cookies or any other tracking mechanisms within the app.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you.
- **Rectify** inaccurate data.
- **Erase** your data — carried out by crypto-shredding, which makes it permanently unreadable.
- **Receive** your data in a portable format.
- **Object** to certain processing.

If your oase belongs to a Plenum, that organisation is the data controller for your profile and content data, and you should as a starting point direct your request to them. In all other cases — and for billing data — contact us at [dpa@oase.app](mailto:dpa@oase.app).

You can also lodge a complaint with the Danish Data Protection Agency, Datatilsynet, at [datatilsynet.dk](https://www.datatilsynet.dk).

## Data retention

Content lives for as long as the oase it was shared in exists, or until it is deleted by a user or the controlling organisation. When it is deleted, the keys are destroyed and the data becomes permanently unreadable. Billing data is kept as described above.
